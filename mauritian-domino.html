Mauritian house rules

Teams. Seats 1 & 3 are partners, seats 2 & 4 are partners, sitting across from one another.

Deal. Double-six set with the double-blank removed (27 tiles), 6 tiles each. The 3 leftover tiles are set aside and never used.

Opening. The first round is opened by whoever holds the lowest double in play — double-one if it was dealt, otherwise double-two, then three, four, five, six. From the next round on, the round's starter (the previous winner) may open with any tile.

Play. Match either open end of the chain. If you can play, you must — passing is only allowed when you have no legal tile.

Domino. First player to empty their hand wins the round for their team; the losing team adds up the pips left in their own hands as penalty points.

Capicú. If the winning domino matches both open ends of the chain — e.g. a 3 on one side and a 5 on the other, and the last tile played is [3|5] — the losing team's penalty is doubled.

Blocked. If all four pass in a row, the team with fewer pips wins; the losing team adds up the pips left in their hands. Tie on pips means no points.

Match. Points are penalties — the first team to reach 101 loses the match.
